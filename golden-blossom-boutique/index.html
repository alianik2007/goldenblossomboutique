CLIENT CARE & CONCIERGE

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about delivery across Morocco, payment options, and care.

What are the delivery times and shipping fees in Morocco?

We provide nationwide express delivery across Morocco:

  • Casablanca & Mohammedia: Same-day or 24h express hand delivery.
  • Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier, Agadir, Fes & other cities: 24 to 48 hours via premium courier (Amana / Aramex).
  • Shipping Fee: Free on all orders over 500 MAD (or 35 MAD flat rate for smaller orders).
Do you offer Cash on Delivery (Paiement à la livraison)?

Yes! We offer Cash on Delivery (Paiement à la livraison) everywhere in Morocco. You can inspect your luxury gift packaging upon receipt and pay the courier directly in cash. We also accept secure online payments via Credit Card, CMI, and Apple Pay.

Is Golden Blossom jewelry genuine gold and water-resistant?

All our jewelry pieces are crafted with 3-micron 18K heavy gold vermeil over recycled 925 sterling silver, which is over 5 times thicker than standard flash plating. They are 100% nickel-free, hypoallergenic, and water-resistant for everyday wear.

How do I find my exact ring or dress size?

You can check our interactive or WhatsApp our Casablanca stylist who will gladly measure or advise you live with photo assistance.

Can I order directly or customize via WhatsApp?

Absolutely. You can click our WhatsApp concierge button or message us directly at +212 780-760188 at any time to place your order with a live styling advisor, request personalized gift messages, or get exact size recommendations for rings and dresses.

What is your exchange and return policy?

We gladly offer 7-day hassle-free exchanges across Morocco. If a ring size or apparel fit isn't perfect, our courier will deliver the replacement right to your doorstep and collect the original item.

ATELIER ARCHIVES

18K Heavy Vermeil Care Guide

Golden Blossom pieces are crafted with 3 microns of solid 18-karat gold over 925 sterling silver. To preserve their luminous sheen for decades:

01

Avoid Harsh Chemicals

Apply perfumes, lotions, and body oils before putting on your jewelry. Let cosmetic products dry completely.

02

Gentle Cleansing

Polish gently using our complimentary micro-fiber polishing cloth. Never use harsh abrasive sonic cleaners.

03

Storage in Velvet

Store individual pieces inside the provided anti-tarnish velvet pouch to prevent surface scratches.

04

Water Resistance

Our heavy vermeil is safe for fresh water showers, but avoid prolonged chlorine pools and mineral hot springs.
